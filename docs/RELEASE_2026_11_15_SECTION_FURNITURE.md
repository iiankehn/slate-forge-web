# Section header/footer release — November 15, 2026

PR [#40](https://github.com/iiankehn/slate-forge-web/pull/40) distinguishes explicitly blank section header/footer text from legacy empty text that inherits. Section tools exposes both choices. Preview, native ODT and document-level Word sections preserve the resolved text, including numbering-only footers. Nested ODT sections use their actual parent scope; nested Word clearing explicitly refuses output. Word also restores document defaults after scoped custom text.

Bare plain-text ODT page-master changes now retain distinct or blank furniture even without an orientation change. Styled/complex furniture and existing unsupported paper, margin, numbering, restart, nesting and native-history designs retain their guarded fallback. HTML, saving, backups and editable sharing retain the optional flags. Older builds may ignore them and inherit text. Originals, backup envelopes, database/session identifiers, origins, private Site, body font choices and mobile safe areas are preserved.
