The accepted direct/resaved PDFs retain all three portrait/landscape/portrait pages, their text/geometry and identical 144-DPI pixels. The ODT seed's XML validates separately. This does not claim cross-format pixel equality. GitHub retains generated DOCX, actual resave, PDFs and the report.

The initial sequential experiment failed native reimport; inspection showed inactive first/even references, unsupported 8-point spacing and removal of `w:noColumnBalance` by Writer. The inactive-reference and spacing representations are repaired. Sequential DOCX export was withdrawn and remains refused; its failed input/resave and specification stay retained. Explicit foreign sequential declarations import faithfully, but absent settings never restore a guessed/private flow. The accepted balanced case is a distinct representation, not a relaxed sequential assertion. See [WORD_SECTION_FLOW.md](WORD_SECTION_FLOW.md).
